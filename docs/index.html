γ-EV Model Explorer

Two mechanistic models of how a bean dipeptide changes the aortic proteome in ApoE-deficient mice, and what three doses can actually tell them apart. Move the controls to see why one receptor is not enough.

01 / the structural claim

One receptor cannot change direction

M1 is a single reversible receptor. M3 is the same model plus a beta-arrestin2 brake on the occupied receptor, which switches on once occupancy passes a threshold. The rate constants here are hand-picked, so read the shape and not the magnitude. The brake threshold below is occupancy, measured in occupied receptors out of 100, not dose.

02 / calibration

Fitting both models to the real fold changes

Four targets, fitted with PyDREAM: VCAM1 at 150 and 500 mg/kg/day, and the mean of the five flip-protein anchors at the same doses. Gaussian likelihood on the log2 scale, uniform priors in log10, 3 chains of 30000 iterations, both converged. Thin lines are posterior draws, thick lines the best fit, points the data with their assumed spread.

M1's best possible fit to the flip proteins is flat at -0.30 for both doses. It is not that M1 fits worse, it is that no parameter values let a single saturating receptor change sign across dose. Delta AIC of 26 favours M3 after paying for its four extra parameters.

03 / identifiability

What three doses can and cannot pin down

How much each fitted parameter of M3 narrowed from its prior once the data were applied. The switch steepness and the induction amplitude are genuinely constrained. The rate constants are not: they sit where the prior put them. This is the honest limit of a three-dose design, and it is the part worth arguing about.

04 / caveats

What this does not yet establish

Three doses cannot separate the rates

Both runs have converged (Gelman-Rubin 1.02 for M3, 1.00 for M1, over 30000 iterations by 3 chains). What the sampling then shows is that the arrestin catalytic rate and the reset rate barely move off their priors. The shape is identified, the kinetics are not.

The error bars are a placeholder

Sigma is fixed at 0.35 log2 units rather than limma's per-protein standard errors, which have not been exported yet.

The Hill coefficient is doing a lot of work

The posterior prefers n between 7 and 15, steeper than a plain arrestin-recruitment step should give. Receptor internalization and recycling are the likely missing mechanism.

VCAM1 recovers and the model does not

The data show -0.48 at 500 against the model's -0.91. That point carries FDR 0.41 in proteomics, and the western blot establishes only that it is lower, not by how much.

Dose units are nominal

Dose enters the model in the same arbitrary units as the binding rates, so the receptor affinity is fitted rather than assumed. The mg/kg/day labels are for orientation.

The readout may be contaminated

Tnnc1 was excluded because troponin is absent from smooth muscle, pointing to cardiac tissue in the aortic arch samples. Histology is pending on the five anchors that remain.
